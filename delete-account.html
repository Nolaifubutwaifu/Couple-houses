Delete your NEST account

NEST by Taiga Projects

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open NEST and tap ··· at the top right.
  2. Scroll to Delete your account.
  3. Type delete in the box and tap Delete my account.

Deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone. You are signed out on that device.

If you cannot open the app

Email support@example.com with the subject Delete my NEST account. Tell us your display name, the name of your nest if it has one, and your partner's display name, so we can find the right account. We may ask a question only the account holder could answer before deleting, and we will confirm within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What is deleted

  • Your display name and date of birth
  • Your sign in identity and any linked sign in method
  • Your answers to the daily questions
  • Your blocks, and your notification subscription

What is kept, and why

  • The shared home. A nest belongs to both people. It freezes, your name is removed from it, and your former partner can still view it. It is not shown on the street.
  • Reports you made, without your name, for up to 12 months after they are resolved, so repeated abuse can be recognised.
  • Error reports and anonymous usage events for up to 90 days, after which they are removed.
