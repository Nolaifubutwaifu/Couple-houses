Privacy Policy

Effective 15 September 2026 · operated by Taiga Projects

NEST is a game for two people who share one home: you play small daily games together, earn coins, and build and decorate a little house under a glass dome. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, where it is kept, who else handles it, and what you can do about it. It is written to meet the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), and it gives everyone, wherever they live, the access and deletion rights described below.

The short version. We keep the least we need to run a shared home for two people: a sign in identity, the name you choose, your date of birth (to check you are sixteen or over), and the house the two of you build. We do not sell your information, we do not show you targeted advertising, and you can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

NEST is operated by Taiga Projects, based in Australia. For anything about your information, write to support@example.com.

2. What we collect, and why

InformationWhy we need it
Account identifier. A random ID created when you start, and, if you choose to sign in with Apple, Google or email, the identifier and email address that provider gives us. To keep you signed in and to give you back your nest when you return.
Display name. The name you ask us to call you. So your partner knows it is you, and so your names can appear together on your home.
Date of birth. To confirm you are at least 16. If you are younger, we do not keep your name or date of birth; we keep only a flag that stops the check being retried.
Your nest. The name of your home, what you have bought and where it stands, coins, streak, your answers to the daily question and trivia duel, and the invite codes used to pair. This is the game. It is shared with your partner, because the two of you build it together.
What you publish to the street, only if you choose to: your home's name, your first names, a one line tagline, and a picture of what is in the house. So other couples can visit your home. You can take it off the street at any time.
Hearts, reports and blocks you make on the street. To count hearts once per person, and to keep the street safe (see our Community Guidelines).
Notification subscription, only if you turn notifications on: a push address issued by your browser or device, and your time zone offset. To tell you when your partner does something in your nest, and to send one daily reminder at a sensible local time.
Technical information. Error reports (what went wrong, on which screen, browser type) and anonymous usage events such as "invite shared" or "first item placed". To find and fix problems and to understand which parts of the game work.
Language setting of your device.To show dates and text sensibly.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and we do not ask for your real name, address or payment details.

3. Where it is kept, and who helps us

We use a small number of service providers who process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles sign in. Your nest is stored in their Sydney, Australia region (ap-southeast-2).
  • Vercel serves the app and these pages, and keeps short lived request logs. If we enable Vercel Web Analytics, it counts visits without cookies.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the typeface and a code library, which means your browser sends them its IP address when it loads them.
  • Apple, Google or our email provider, only if you choose to sign in that way or receive a sign in code by email.
  • Browser push services (such as Apple, Google or Mozilla), only if you turn notifications on. They receive the notification to deliver it.

Some of these providers may process information outside Australia, for example in the United States. We choose providers that protect information to a standard comparable to the Australian Privacy Principles.

4. What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not use your information for targeted or behavioural advertising. If we ever show advertising, it will not be based on a profile of you, and this policy will be updated before it starts.
  • We do not show anything you have not published to anyone except your partner.

5. Your partner, and the shared home

A nest belongs to both people in it. Your partner can see your display name, your answers once you have both answered, and everything in the house. If either of you leaves or deletes their account, the nest freezes: nothing more can be added, and both of you can still look at it. Deleting your account removes your name, birthday and your own answers; the furniture and streak remain, because your partner built them too. The full reasoning is in our Terms.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and nest information: for as long as your account exists.
  • After you delete your account: your personal details are erased immediately. A frozen nest you shared stays viewable by your former partner, without your name.
  • Reports: up to 12 months after they are resolved, so repeated abuse can be recognised.
  • Error reports and usage events: up to 90 days.
  • Guest accounts that are never paired and unused for 12 months may be removed.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction. Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it.
  • Deletion. Delete your account in the app under ··· → Delete your account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Street. Take your home off the street at any time from the Street tab.
  • Notifications. Turn them off in the app's settings or in your browser or device settings.
  • Complaints. Write to us first and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au, or the data protection authority where you live.

8. Age

NEST is for people aged 16 and over. We check date of birth at sign up and do not knowingly keep information about anyone younger. If you believe someone under 16 is using NEST, please tell us at support@example.com.

9. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections. The database enforces who may read and change what, so a person can only reach the nest they belong to. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.
