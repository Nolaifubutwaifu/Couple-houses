Child Safety Standards

Effective 15 September 2026 · Taiga Projects

NEST has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material, grooming, and any content or behaviour that sexualises minors.

Our standards

  • NEST is only for people aged 16 and over. Date of birth is checked at sign up, and a failed check cannot be retried by changing the date.
  • NEST has no private messaging, no photo or video uploads, and no way to contact strangers. What is published to the street is limited to a home's name, first names, a short tagline and in game furniture, and links, emails and phone numbers are refused.
  • Any content or account connected to CSAE is removed and the accounts are closed.
  • We report apparent child sexual abuse material and exploitation to the appropriate authorities, including the Australian Centre to Counter Child Exploitation (ACCCE) and, where relevant, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), and we cooperate with law enforcement.

How to report a concern

  • In the app: open the home on the street and tap Report.
  • By email: support@example.com with the subject Child safety report. Child safety reports are prioritised.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (000 in Australia). You can also report directly to the ACCCE at accce.gov.au/report.

Designated contact

Our child safety point of contact can be reached at support@example.com, and is able to speak to our CSAE prevention practices and compliance.
