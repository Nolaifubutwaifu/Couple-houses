Common questions

How do my partner and I get into the same nest?

One of you taps Start a nest, adds a name and birthday, and chooses I want to invite my partner. Send them the invite link or the six character code. They open the link (or choose My partner sent me a code), and then the first person confirms it is really them. Codes work once and last seven days.

My partner says the code does not work.

Check the code has not expired or already been used, and that it is typed exactly (there is no letter O, I or L, and no digit 0 or 1). On the invite screen, New code makes a fresh one and cancels the old one.

I cleared my browser, or changed phones, and my nest is gone.

A guest account lives in the browser that created it. If that browser still has it, opening NEST there brings you straight back. If it was cleared, the account cannot be recovered, but the nest still exists: ask your partner to invite you again, or write to us from the address you used and we will help where we can.

How do the coins and daily limits work?

Today's question pays when both of you have answered, once a day, and more the longer your streak runs. The trivia duel pays once a day, and memory match twice. The limits are what keep a home a record of time spent together rather than a race.

How do I leave a nest?

Open ··· at the top of the app, then Leave this nest. The nest freezes for both of you: nothing is deleted, neither of you can change it again, and you can both start a new one.

How do I delete my account?

Open ···, scroll to Delete your account, type delete and confirm. It happens immediately. More detail, including what happens if you cannot sign in, is on the account deletion page.

How do I report a home on the street, or block it?

Open the home from the Street tab and tap Report or Block. A reported home disappears from your street straight away and a person reviews it. Read what is and is not allowed in our Community Guidelines.

Notifications are not arriving.

Check they are on under ··· → Notifications and allowed for this site in your browser or device settings. On iPhone and iPad, web notifications only work after adding NEST to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen).

Safety

If you believe someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services (000 in Australia). To report a child safety concern, see our Child Safety Standards.
