Community Guidelines

Effective 15 September 2026

The street is where couples show each other the homes they have built. It works because it is kind. These guidelines apply to everything you publish: your nest's name, your tagline, and anything else other people can see.

Not allowed

  • Hate, harassment, threats or bullying, including towards a group or a real person
  • Sexual content, or anything that sexualises minors (see our Child Safety Standards)
  • Violence, self harm encouragement, or illegal activity
  • Personal information: links, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses or social handles, yours or anyone's
  • Spam, advertising, scams or impersonation

Some of this is refused automatically when you publish. The rest is handled by reports.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report a home from its page on the street. It disappears from your street immediately.
  • Block hides a home from you, and can be undone.
  • When several different people report the same home, it is hidden from everyone until a person reviews it.
  • We aim to review reports within 24 hours.

What happens when a rule is broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may remove the home from the street, reset its name or tagline, suspend publishing, or close the accounts involved. Anything involving harm to a child is reported to the authorities.
